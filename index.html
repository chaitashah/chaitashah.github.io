Hello.

Chaitali Shah is the Founder and Creative Director of Bobaplans, an online art business specializing in large-scale framed canvas artwork.

With a background in Computer Science and Engineering, she began her career in IT, working with banking and financial-services clients in the UK and United States, including Barclays and SunTrust Bank. She later transitioned into entrepreneurship, bringing together her technical, business, and creative interests through Bobaplans.

At Bobaplans, she leads the creative direction of the business, with a focus on design, color, product development, customization, and customer experience.

When she’s not wandering through museums, discovering great restaurants, or taking nature walks, she’s busy working with colors in her studio.
